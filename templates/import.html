Two ways to import — we don't know your Airtable format yet

Option A — CSV upload (below): Export your candidates table from Airtable as CSV. The importer tries common column name variations but your column names may not match. Fastest if you share your Airtable field names — we can hardcode the exact mapping in 5 minutes.
Option B — Pull direct from Airtable API: Since your AIRTABLE_API_KEY and AIRTABLE_BASE_ID are already set, we can build a one-click import that reads your base directly — no export needed, no column name guessing. Best long-term option. To enable it, share your historical candidates table name and we'll add a second env var AIRTABLE_IMPORT_TABLE.
