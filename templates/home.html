TopSDRs Candidate Screener

This tool automates the first pass of SDR candidate screening. When someone fills out the Webflow intake form, their profile is automatically scored against the TopSDRs rubric using Gemini. High-scoring candidates surface in the outreach queue so Andrew can send a single-click email with a Calendly link. Every sent email is logged to Airtable.

CandidatesAll inbounds, sorted by recency. Shows each candidate's assigned tier.
LabelReview candidates one by one and give them a 1–10 rating. Your labels train the kNN similarity model over time.
OutreachStrong Intro and Weak Intro candidates who haven't been contacted yet. Click Compose & Send to fire the email via Resend.
Data FlowVisual step-by-step of how a candidate moves from Webflow form to Airtable CRM entry.
Candidate ScorerHow the rubric + kNN scoring works, how to train it, backtest it, and correct it when wrong.
